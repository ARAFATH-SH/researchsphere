Call for papers ↗

Scope

The conference invites original research on digital tools for health monitoring and diagnosis, including wearable and passive sensing, mobile health interventions, explainable and trustworthy machine learning for clinical decision support, health data privacy, and evaluation of digital health systems in low-resource settings.

Topics include, but are not limited to: digital phenotyping, vision and eye health, human factors of screen use, federated learning on health data, and reproducibility of clinical AI.
